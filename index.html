Your pool should be the easy part

Between storms, heat and gum trees, pools around Sydney, the Coast and Newcastle take a beating. These are the jobs we fix every week.

Green after every storm

Summer storms and heat can turn clear water green overnight. Regular balancing keeps algae out, and if it's already green we'll bring it back.

Leaves, gumnuts, blocked baskets

Gum trees drop all year. Full baskets starve your pump and cloud the water. We clear the lot every visit.

An interior that's seen better days

Stains, rough patches and flaking paint. A new epoxy, pebble or glass bead interior makes an old pool look brand new.

Gear that's playing up

A noisy pump, a chlorinator that's stopped working, a filter that won't clear. We service, repair and replace it.

Too many trades to chase

A cleaner, a repairer, someone for the paving. Or one crew for the whole job. That's us.

Selling or renting out?

A sparkling pool lifts your listing photos and first impressions. We'll get it looking its best, fast.

Good to know

How much does a pool service cost?

Regular care is $79 a fortnight or $90 a month, and a one-off full service is $110. All prices include GST, plus chemicals at what your pool actually uses. Refer friends and your regular price drops as low as $67.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Stay because you're happy with the water, not because you signed something. You can pause or cancel any time.

Do I need to be home?

Usually not. As long as we can get to the pool, we'll do the job and let you know when we're done.

My pool is green. Can you fix it?

Yes. We'll do a recovery clean to bring it back, then keep it clear with regular visits. You'll get the price before we start.

How long does a new interior take?

An epoxy interior is about three days on site, then seven days to cure before we fill. Pebble and glass bead depend on the pool, and your quote includes an exact timeline.

Which interior is right for my pool?

Epoxy gives a fresh, glossy colour and is great value. Pebblecrete is tough and natural-looking. Glass bead adds sparkle and feels smooth underfoot. We'll look at your pool and recommend the best fit.

How do I get 10% off?

Join the Lukewarm List at the bottom of this page, or tick the box when you book. We'll email you a code for 10% off your first booking. It can't be combined with the half-price first visit.

Do you come to my area?

We cover everywhere from Sydney to Newcastle, including the Central Coast, Lake Macquarie, Hornsby, the Hills and the Northern Beaches. If you're not sure, just ask.
