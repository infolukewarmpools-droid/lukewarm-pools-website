Good to know

Questions, answered

We cover everywhere from Sydney to Newcastle: the Northern Beaches, Hills District, Hornsby, the Central Coast, Lake Macquarie and Newcastle.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Stay because you're happy with the water, not because you signed something. Pause or cancel any time.

Do I need to be home?

Usually not. As long as we can get to the pool, we'll do the job and let you know when we're done.

My pool is green. Can you fix it?

Yes. We'll do a recovery clean to bring it back, then keep it clear with regular visits. You'll get the price before we start.

How long does a new interior take?

An epoxy interior is about three days on site, then seven days to cure before we fill. Pebble and glass bead depend on the pool, and your quote includes an exact timeline.

Do you do small jobs?

Yes. Gate latches, pump swaps, leak checks and small paving repairs are all welcome. Bigger fixes get a fixed quote first.
